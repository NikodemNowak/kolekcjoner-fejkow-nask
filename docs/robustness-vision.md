# Robustness of the image signals to new variants

Gallery: 289 corpus images (≥ 120 px) from `b_vision_gated`; 73 of them yield creative regions. Queries: 150 originals × 9 synthetic transformations (seed 0). A hit is the original or a near-identical gallery copy (PDQ ≤ 20). Hash scores are Hamming distances (lower = closer); SigLIP is cosine. `+regions` adds creative-region crops on both sides (best pair counts). Generated by `scripts/b_robustness.py`; numbers only.
